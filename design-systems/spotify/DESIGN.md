# Spotify Encore Design Package

## Visual Theme

Dark music-native editorial surface. The interface should feel like a listening history console, not a generic SaaS site. Use black and near-black foundations, bright Spotify green for action and signal, and album-art color only as content texture. Keep the app name clear and preserve the existing "not affiliated with Spotify AB" framing in the site footer.

## Color

Spotify green is the only accent. Use it for primary actions, active filters, graph signals, and small proof marks. Pair it with black, near-black, white, and neutral gray. Avoid purple, blue, beige, brass, glassy multicolor gradients, and unrelated accent colors.

## Typography

Use a direct sans-serif stack. Headings are heavy and compact, but letter spacing stays normal. Body copy is short, high contrast, and pragmatic. Avoid decorative serif emphasis and avoid long manifesto blocks.

## Layout

Use editorial bands with clear section changes. Prefer asymmetric hero composition: copy on one side, data/media plate on the other. Repeated cards use 8px radius or less. Album artwork and artist imagery should sit in clean shelves, matrices, or rows. Do not overlay text on Spotify artwork.

## Motion

Motion is restrained: section reveal, active filter state, button feedback. No autoplaying decorative motion. Respect reduced motion. Sticky navigation should stay usable and should not cover focused content.

## Imagery

Use product-like plates: ranking lists, listening fingerprints, snapshot timelines, taste overlap matrices, and cover grids. If Spotify artwork or metadata appears, keep it legible, uncropped, and accompanied by Spotify attribution elsewhere on the surface.

## Anti-patterns

- No generic dashboard stock art.
- No fake user counts, fake revenue numbers, or fake testimonials.
- No text overlays on album artwork.
- No Spotify logo placed on busy media.
- No decorative gradient orbs.
- No more than one accent color.

## Agent Prompt Guide

Create editorial landing-page image plates for a Spotify-connected analytics app. Build dark surfaces, clean grids, ranking bars, compact music metadata rows, and cover-art shelves. Use Spotify green as the only accent. Keep images label-free or use non-readable marks only. Do not invent readable marketing copy, watermarks, or fake Spotify UI.
