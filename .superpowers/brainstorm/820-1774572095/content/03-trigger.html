What makes you want to check your stats?

Understanding the "why" helps us design the right first impression

A

"Did my taste change?"

You've been listening to new stuff and want to see if the numbers reflect it

B

"I want to show someone"

Share your music identity with a friend — it's a social thing

C

"Curious about the data"

You just like seeing your listening habits turned into charts and rankings

D

"Routine check-in"

Got a notification or it's been a while — casual browse

E

"Just make it cool"

No specific goal — you want it to look great and surface something interesting on its own
