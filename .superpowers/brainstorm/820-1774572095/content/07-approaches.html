A. Hero + Highlights Feed

Big #1 artist hero at top with album art, then a row of highlight cards (top track, top album, biggest shift). Below: "Three Versions" and "Hall of Fame" as expandable sections.

Pros

  • Instant identity — you see your #1 immediately
  • Clean hierarchy, no overwhelm
  • Very shareable hero section

Cons

  • Less data density on first view
  • #1 might not change often — could feel stale

B. Identity Triptych

"Three Versions of You" as the hero. Three panels showing your music identity across time ranges, with top artist art in each. Hall of Fame stats as a compact row below. No traditional rankings list on the home page.

Pros

  • Leads with the strongest concept
  • Always feels fresh (3 different views)
  • Extremely shareable — tells a story

Cons

  • Rankings lists move to sub-pages
  • Novel layout — users might expect a traditional list

C. Curated Bento

Mixed-size cards blending rankings, insights, and stats into one visual grid. Largest card is your #1 artist, smaller cards for highlights, streaks, and movements. Everything on one scrollable page.

Pros

  • Visually dynamic — no two cards the same
  • Everything visible at a glance
  • Flexible — easy to add/remove cards later

Cons

  • Harder to scan for specific info
  • Can feel busy if not carefully curated
